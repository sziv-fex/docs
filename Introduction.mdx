# Welcome to FEX Developer Portal

Build wallet-based payment experiences with FEX using step-by-step guides, API references, and tools for merchant integrations.

FEX is an alternative payment method that lets customers pay directly from funded wallet balances, without relying on traditional card infrastructure. The platform supports merchant payments, peer-to-peer transfers, bill splitting, and controlled spending through wallets and allocations.

## What you can do with Fex Payments

- **Merchant payment flows** for POS, web, and mobile
- **Wallet-based checkout experiences** without card rails
- **Refund and transaction workflows** for post-payment operations
- **Secure integrations** with authentication and webhooks

## Platform highlights

- **Wallet-based payments** for direct, card-free transactions
- **Bill splitting and group payments** for shared spending use cases
- **Merchant rewards support** built into the payment experience
- **Strong security** with multi-factor authentication and encryption
- **Allocations system** for controlled and rule-based spending
