## Welcome to FEX Developer Portal

Integrate wallet-based payments with FEX using step-by-step guides, API references, and merchant integration tools.

FEX Payments is an alternative payment method that enables users to pay directly from funded wallet balances, without traditional credit card infrastructure. The platform supports peer-to-peer transfers, bill splitting, merchant payments, and controlled spending through wallets and allocations.

## What you can build

- Merchant payment flows for POS, web, and mobile
- Wallet-based checkout experiences
- Refund and transaction workflows
- Secure integrations with webhooks and authentication

## Platform highlights

- **Wallet-based payments** for direct, card-free transactions
- **Bill splitting and group payments** for shared spending use cases
- **Merchant rewards support** built into the payment experience
- **Strong security** with multi-factor authentication and encryption
- **Allocations system** for controlled and rule-based spending
