# **Overview **

**Fex Payments** is a digital cash management application designed for seamless financial interaction among friends, family, and groups. It simplifies P2P payments, bill splitting, expense tracking, and direct merchant payments, functioning as an **Alternative Payment Method (APM)** without traditional credit cards.

The platform uses a "Wallet" system, which users fund via cash deposits or bank transfers (ACH, RTP).

## **Key Features:**

1. **Instant Wallet Transfers:** Friction-less P2P payments and group settlements.
2. **Intelligent Bill Splitting:** Algorithms simplify dividing expenses, supporting uneven splits.
3. **Merchant Loyalty Support:** Tracks and redeems merchant rewards.
4. **Social Activity Feed:** Transparent view of financial interactions.
5. **Robust Security:** Multi-factor authentication and data encryption.

The core is the **Wallet** and the **Allocations** system—digital, categorized "envelopes" for granular control and budgeting (e.g., "Groceries"). Allocations support constraints like specific merchants or expiration dates. Payments prioritize funds from the most restrictive Allocation first.
